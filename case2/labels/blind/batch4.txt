#550 Q: How many weeks are in a year?
A: There are **52 weeks** in a standard year.

Here’s the breakdown:
*   A standard year has **365 days**.
*   There are **7 days** in a week.
*   365 ÷ 7 = **52 weeks** with **1 day** remaining.

In a **leap year** (366 days), there are **52 weeks** with **2 days** remaining.
