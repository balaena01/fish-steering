#77 Q: Which gas do humans need to breathe in to stay alive?
A: Humans need to breathe in **oxygen** ($O_2$) to stay alive.

While we often think of breathing as taking in air, the specific gas required for cellular respiration is oxygen. When we inhale, our lungs extract oxygen from the air, which then travels to our cells. Inside the cells, oxygen is used to break down glucose and produce energy (in the form of ATP) that powers all bodily functions. Without a continuous supply of oxygen, the body cannot generate this energy, leading to cell death and eventually death within minutes.
